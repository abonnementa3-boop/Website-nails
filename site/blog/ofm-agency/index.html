OFM agency: how they really work.

Updated November 24, 2026 · 2 min read

In shortAn OFM agency is a team organized around one job: turning a creator's content into revenue. They come in three models (chat-only, full-service, launch), and the difference between a good and a bad one is almost always the number of accounts each person handles.

Who works inside an OFM agency

  • Chatters, working in shifts to cover every time zone.
  • A social media team, planning and posting on the creator's accounts.
  • Traffic operators, bringing new visitors to the page.
  • An account manager, the creator's contact, who reads the numbers and sets prices.

In a small agency one person may hold several of these roles. That's fine for a handful of creators. It stops working when that person follows dozens.

The three models

ModelWhat the agency doesWhat stays with you
Chat-onlyReplies to fans and sells paid contentSocial media, traffic, often pricing
Full-serviceChatting, social media, pricing, traffic on top of yoursCreating the content
LaunchBuilds an account from zero, then runs itCreating the content, on a lower starting share

Naylis works on the full-service model for established creators, and on custom terms for new ones.

What a normal split looks like

Chat-only agencies tend to sit around 20 to 30%. Full-service agencies around 40 to 50%. Launch deals can go higher at first and should be renegotiable. All of it applies after the 20% OnlyFans keeps. Worked example in OnlyFans agency percentages.

How a good OFM agency sets prices

Pricing is where agencies differ most, and where the money is. A common mistake is chasing a high open rate on paid messages. When more than 40% of fans open a paid message, it's priced too low: you sell a lot, below value. At Naylis, scripts and prices are tuned to stay under that 40% ceiling.

Real team or one-person operation?

Ask five questions and listen to how precise the answers are:

  1. How many chatters cover my account, and on which hours?
  2. How many creators does my account manager follow?
  3. Who posts on my socials, and how often?
  4. What do you do to generate traffic that doesn't come from me?
  5. How do I leave, and what happens to my account when I do?

Vague answers usually mean there's no team behind the logo. The warning signs are listed in OnlyFans agency scams.

Is an OFM agency right for you?

It fits if messages go unanswered for hours, if your income hasn't moved in months, or if you spend more time managing than creating. If that's you, the application takes two minutes. If you're still comparing, start with what OFM means.

Frequently asked questions

What is an OFM agency?

An OnlyFans management agency: a team that runs the chatting, social media, traffic and pricing of a creator's account in exchange for a share of revenue.

How much does an OFM agency take?

Usually between 30% and 60% depending on the services, after the 20% OnlyFans keeps.

Do OFM agencies work with beginners?

Some do. Terms differ: the agency invests more upfront, so the creator's share can be lower at first and should be renegotiable.

Can I leave an OFM agency?

Yes, as the contract states. Check the notice period before signing and make sure the account stays in your name.
