Definition

What is OFM? OnlyFans management, explained.

Updated November 24, 2026 · 2 min read

In shortOFM means OnlyFans management: a team runs the business side of a creator's account (fan conversations, paid-content sales, social media, traffic, pricing) while the creator makes the content. An OFM agency is paid with a percentage of revenue, not a fee.

OFM meaning

OFM is short for OnlyFans management. You'll see it written as OFM, OFM agency, OnlyFans management agency or simply OnlyFans agency. They all describe the same thing: people who operate a creator's account for her and take a share of what it earns.

The idea behind it: making content and selling content are two different jobs. One needs creativity and time on camera. The other needs someone online at every hour, an eye on the numbers and a working knowledge of social platforms.

What OFM covers

AreaWhat it means in practice
ChattingReplying to fans and selling paid content in direct messages, day and night.
Social mediaPlanning, posting and monitoring the accounts that bring subscribers.
TrafficBringing new people to the page, on top of what the creator brings.
PricingSetting subscription and content prices, deciding on offers.
TrackingReading the stats and adjusting scripts and strategy.

Not every OFM agency does all five. Many only chat. Compare the list of services before you compare percentages.

The OFM vocabulary

  • Chatter: the person replying to fans on the creator's behalf. See OnlyFans chatting.
  • PPV: pay-per-view, a paid message a fan unlocks.
  • Open rate: the share of fans who buy a paid message among those who received it.
  • Script: a written sales framework chatters follow.
  • Traffic: the visitors landing on the creator's page.
  • Split: how revenue is shared between creator and agency.

How OFM is paid

An OFM agency keeps a percentage of the creator's revenue, most often between 30% and 60%, after the 20% OnlyFans keeps. There should be no entry fee. The details are in what percentage an OnlyFans agency takes.

OFM from the creator's side

For a creator, OFM is worth it when the agency grows sales enough that she keeps more than she did alone, while working less. It isn't when the agency takes its cut and leaves her to post, bring the traffic and answer the hard messages herself.

Frequently asked questions

What does OFM stand for?

OnlyFans management: the running of a creator's OnlyFans account by an agency or a manager.

Is OFM the same as an OnlyFans agency?

Yes. "OFM agency", "OnlyFans management agency" and "OnlyFans agency" are used for the same activity.

Does OFM include creating content?

No. The creator makes the content. OFM covers selling it, the fan conversations, social media and traffic.

How do OFM agencies make money?

They keep a percentage of the creator's revenue. A serious agency charges no upfront fee.
