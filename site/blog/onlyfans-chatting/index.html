OnlyFans chatting: where the revenue is made.

Updated November 23, 2026 · 2 min read

In shortChatting is the direct-message conversation with fans, where paid content is sold. It's the main revenue source of an OnlyFans account and it needs someone present 24/7, which is the main reason creators work with an agency.

What is OnlyFans chatting?

A fan pays a subscription to access your page. But most of what he spends afterwards goes through direct messages: paid messages (PPV, for pay-per-view), custom requests, tips. Chatting is all of that conversation and selling work.

Why you can't keep it up alone

A working account gets messages at every hour, from fans in every time zone. A message left unanswered for six hours is a lost sale. Keeping that pace alone, every day, on top of creating content, never lasts long.

What a chatter does

  • Replies fast, in the creator's voice.
  • Knows each fan: what he likes, what he already bought, when he's online.
  • Offers the right content at the right time, at the right price.
  • Follows up with fans who went quiet.

Sales scripts

A script isn't a text read aloud. It's a framework: how to open, how to bring up a piece of content, how to handle a no, how to offer the next one. Good scripts are written for one specific creator and corrected when the numbers drop.

The 40% open-rate rule

The open rate of a paid message is the share of fans who buy it among those who received it. You might think higher is better. It's the opposite: when more than 40% of fans open a paid message, its price is too low. You sell a lot, below value.

At Naylis, scripts and prices are tuned to stay under that 40% ceiling and sell each piece of content at its real value.

Frequently asked questions

What is an OnlyFans chatter?

A trained person who replies to fans in direct messages on a creator's behalf and offers paid content.

What is a PPV on OnlyFans?

A paid message (pay-per-view): the fan pays to unlock the content.

What open rate should paid content aim for?

At Naylis we aim for a maximum of 40%. Above that, the content is priced too low.
