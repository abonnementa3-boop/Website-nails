Does an OnlyFans agency need to be in the US?

No. OnlyFans is a global platform and most agencies work remotely, with teams spread across time zones. What a US creator needs isn't a US address. It's coverage of US hours and English that sounds right to an American fan.

Be careful with agencies that only claim to be "US-based" as a selling point. Ask the practical questions below.

1. Coverage of US hours

Most spending on a US-facing account happens in the evening, Eastern to Pacific. That's a six-hour window that moves across the country, and it falls in the middle of the night in Europe. Ask which hours are staffed, in your time zone, and by how many chatters. "24/7" should mean shifts. Naylis runs chatting 24 hours a day, seven days a week, for exactly this reason.

2. English that sounds American

Fans notice when the tone is off. Ask how chatters are trained, whether scripts are written for your voice, and ask to see sample conversations. More on this in OnlyFans chatting.

3. Payouts stay yours

OnlyFans pays the account holder. Your account should be in your name, with your email and your bank details, and the agency's share should be settled afterwards as the contract says. An agency asking to put its own bank account on your profile is a reason to walk away.

4. Taxes

As a US creator you're generally treated as self-employed, and OnlyFans reports creator earnings to the IRS. The agency's share is typically a business expense, so keep the invoices or statements. Rules depend on your state and situation: talk to a tax professional before you sign, not after.

5. The contract

  • The percentage and the base it applies to (before or after the OnlyFans fee).
  • The term and the notice period to leave.
  • Who owns the content and the account (you).
  • Which law governs the contract, if the agency is abroad.

Have it read by someone independent if any clause is unclear.

What US creators usually pay

The same ranges as everywhere: 20 to 30% for chat-only, 40 to 50% for full service, more for a launch from zero, all after the 20% OnlyFans keeps. See OnlyFans agency percentages for the math in dollars.
